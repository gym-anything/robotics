Groups (keys 1–7):

Each key is one command: the selected motor's position target becomes its measured position ± step (Shift ×4, Alt ×0.2), every other channel keeps its last value, then physics runs for the duration. A target outside the joint's limits is refused and nothing runs. Unitree LowCmd semantics: tau = kp (q* − q) + kd (dq* − dq) + tau_ff, within the motor's torque–speed envelope. Closing or opening a hand sets that hand's five finger motors (not the thumb's rotation) to their closed or open limit.

Keys command the robot only; the truck moves only when the robot's hands and feet move its controls by contact. Every command renders the head camera in an Isaac render worker (about 1.5 s when warm; about 40 s when cold, at the first command and after 2 minutes without an image), and physics ran 6–7.6 wall seconds per simulated second in testing on a shared host. Closing this page disconnects only; the session keeps running.
